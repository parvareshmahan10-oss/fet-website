Structure of

STQ-77

The STQ-77 is a validated 77-item questionnaire that measures the neurochemical dynamics underlying your temperament. It was developed from decades of psychophysiological research and directly maps to the FET model — each score corresponding to a specific neurotransmitter ensemble.

Unlike popular questionnaires that ask what you prefer or how you think, the STQ-77 measures the formal-dynamical properties of your behaviour — how long you can sustain effort, how quickly you integrate new information, where your attention naturally orients.

The Connection

Your neurochemistry shapes
the world you build around yourself

FET proposes a directional relationship: your temperament — the neurochemical dynamics measured by the STQ-77 — predicts the kind of environment you naturally construct. Someone with high Probabilistic Processing tends to surround themselves with books and complex stimuli. Someone with high Sensation Seeking builds an environment rich in physical variety. The SEP-STQ tests whether this prediction holds — across cultures, languages, and backgrounds.

Assessment Two

Specialized Extended
Phenotype
SEP-STQ

The SEP-STQ measures the environment you construct around yourself — what evolutionary biologists call the extended phenotype. Just as a beaver's dam is an expression of its genetics, the spaces, activities, and relationships you build are expressions of your neurochemical temperament.

This questionnaire was designed to test a core prediction of FET: that temperament does not only regulate behaviour — it shapes the entire environmental niche a person creates. If the correlation holds across cultures, it provides powerful evidence that temperament is biological and universal, not merely cultural.

The SEP-STQ is currently being adapted and validated in Arabic and Persian — languages that have been significantly underrepresented in temperament research. Your participation directly contributes to this process.
