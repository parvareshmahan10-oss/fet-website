The STQ-77 is a validated 77-item questionnaire measuring the neurochemical dynamics underlying temperament. Unlike popular personality tests, it measures the formal-dynamical properties of behaviour — how long you sustain effort, how quickly you integrate information, where your attention naturally orients.

Your participation matters. Every response contributes to ongoing cross-cultural validation research across Arabic, Persian, and English-speaking populations.

FET proposes that temperament — the neurochemical dynamics measured by the STQ-77 — predicts the kind of environment you naturally construct. The SEP-STQ tests whether this prediction holds across cultures and languages.

Assessment Two

Specialized
Extended Phenotype
SEP-STQ

The SEP-STQ measures the environment you construct around yourself — what evolutionary biologists call the extended phenotype. Just as a beaver's dam is an expression of its genetics, the spaces, activities, and relationships you build are expressions of your neurochemical temperament.

This questionnaire tests a core prediction of FET: that temperament shapes the entire environmental niche a person creates — not only their behaviour.
